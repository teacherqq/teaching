📗 第一、二課 Idioms 1–2 點擊聽讀音 · tap to listen

天南地北
tiān nán dì běi — all over the place; chatting about anything and everything
老朋友天南地北地聊了整個晚上。Old friends chatted about everything under the sun all night.
行行出狀元
háng háng chū zhuàng yuán — there is a leader in every industry
只要肯努力，行行出狀元。Work hard and every trade has its master.
有朝一日
yǒu zhāo yī rì — someday, one day
只要堅持夢想，有朝一日你一定會成功。Keep chasing your dream and you will succeed one day.
暢所欲言
chàng suǒ yù yán — to speak freely, without holding back
這裏氣氛輕鬆，大家可以暢所欲言。The atmosphere is relaxed — everyone can speak freely.
深思熟慮
shēn sī shú lǜ — careful deliberation; think it through
經過深思熟慮，他終於決定辭職創業。After careful deliberation he decided to start his own business.

📘 第三課 Idioms 3 點擊聽讀音 · tap to listen

興致勃勃
xìng zhì bó bó — bubbling over with enthusiasm
同學們興致勃勃地討論這次旅行的計劃。The students discussed the trip plan with great enthusiasm.
夢寐以求
mèng mèi yǐ qiú — something you long for even in your dreams
這份工作是他夢寐以求的機會。This job is the opportunity he has always dreamed of.
左右為難
zuǒ yòu wéi nán — hard to choose; caught in the middle
一邊是朋友，一邊是家人，讓他左右為難。Friends on one side, family on the other — he's in a dilemma.
兩全其美
liǎng quán qí měi — the best of both worlds
這樣做既能省錢又能省時間，真是兩全其美。It saves both money and time — the best of both worlds.
舉棋不定
jǔ qí bú dìng — hesitant; in two minds (like a chess player)
面對兩個選擇，他一直舉棋不定。Faced with two options, he kept hesitating.

📙 拓展成語 More Idioms 小冊子詞語表 · from the booklet

無所適從
wú suǒ shì cóng — not knowing what to do or whom to follow
爸爸要我洗車，媽媽叫我擦窗戶，弄得我無所適從。Dad wants one thing, Mum another — I don't know which to do.
一籌莫展
yì chóu mò zhǎn — can find no way out; at a complete loss
面對這個難題，大家都一籌莫展。Faced with this problem, everyone was at a loss.
先公而後私
xiān gōng ér hòu sī — put public benefit before personal gain
他做事一向先公而後私。He always puts the public good before himself.
有商有量
yǒu shāng yǒu liàng — to discuss things and decide together
遇到分歧時，一家人應該有商有量。When opinions differ, a family should discuss it together.
模棱兩可
mó léng liǎng kě — ambiguous; not giving a clear answer
他的回答模棱兩可，讓人猜不透。His answer was ambiguous — nobody could tell what he really thought.

💬 句式仿寫

Sentence Patterns — 仿照例句寫一寫 Write your own
為了 …… ，……。
in order to (purpose) — 為了 + 目的
  • 為了忘記學習上的煩惱，我每天都花不少時間在網上玩遊戲、聊天。In order to forget my study worries, I spend a lot of time playing games and chatting online every day.
  • 為了達到他的目標，小明不管風吹雨打，每天都跑1000米。To reach his goal, Xiao Ming runs 1000 metres every day, whatever the weather.
…… 越來越 ……
more and more (increasing degree)
  • 我發現我越來越喜歡中文了。I find that I like Chinese more and more.
  • 自從上中學後，我的煩惱越來越多。Since starting secondary school, my worries have grown more and more.
…… 比 …… + 形容詞
comparing: A is more … than B
  • 我們現在要學習的科目比以前多了。We now study more subjects than before.
  • 他的中文說得比以前好了。His Chinese is better than before.
自從 …… ，……。
since (a point in time) — 自從 + 時間點
  • 自從上中學後，我的煩惱多了不少。Since starting secondary school, I've had quite a few more worries.
  • 自從失業後，我一直沒有找到工作。Since losing my job, I still haven't found work.
由於 …… ，……。
because of (reason) — 由於 + 原因
  • 由於老師的教學進度很快，很多東西我都聽不懂。Because the teacher goes very fast, I can't understand many things.
  • 由於缺乏溝通，兩兄弟的感情漸漸疏遠。Due to a lack of communication, the two brothers have gradually grown apart.
…… 。此外，還 ……。
besides; in addition — 引出另一點
  • 上了中學後，要學習的科目比以前多了。此外，還有很多功課要做。At secondary school there are more subjects than before. In addition, there is a lot of homework.
  • 我每星期要上中文課。此外，還要完成詞語練習。I have Chinese lessons every week. Besides that, I also have to finish vocabulary exercises.
